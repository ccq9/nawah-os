# titles a client chose. A quote, a control byte, a newline, a bidi control, a line separator and
# a lead byte with nothing after it are named [U+XXXX] between the quotes; a title whose names do
# not fit is cut at a whole character or name and marked cut=<k>; a title longer than TITLE_MAX
# is stored cut at a character boundary; a plain title reads as it always did.
