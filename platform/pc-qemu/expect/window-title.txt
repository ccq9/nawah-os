# TST-M5-WINDOW-TITLE (WP-97, display.md v1.14): the compositor's own narrator lines for window
# titles a client chose. A quote, the control characters (C0, DEL, C1), the line and paragraph
# separators, the bidi formatting characters and every byte that does not begin a valid UTF-8
# character are named [U+XXXX] between the quotes; a title whose names do not fit in 96 bytes is
# cut at a whole character or name; a title longer than TITLE_MAX (32 bytes) keeps the whole
# units that fit (a character the limit falls inside is left out; a character that ends at the
# limit and a byte that is no character stay); cut=<k> at the end of the line counts the
# units of the title as sent that the line does not carry; a plain title reads as it always did.
